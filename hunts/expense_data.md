# Hunt: Paid AI seats in expense and procurement data

**Why:** Paid personal subscriptions expensed back to the company are shadow AI with a receipt. They also tell you which teams want AI badly enough to pay for it, which is where a sanctioned option should go first.

## Search terms

Search expense line items, corporate card transactions and procurement requests for merchant names and descriptions containing:

`OpenAI`, `ChatGPT`, `Anthropic`, `Claude`, `Google One AI`, `Gemini`, `Perplexity`, `Midjourney`, `Otter`, `Fireflies`, `Fathom`, `Read AI`, `Cursor`, `GitHub Copilot`, `Jasper`, `Grammarly`, `Notion AI`, `Poe`, `x.ai`, `Grok`.

## What to record

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Department / cost center | Shows where demand is |
| Tool and plan (personal vs team) | Personal plans usually allow training on inputs unless the user opted out |
| Monthly cost | Builds the business case for a sanctioned enterprise plan |

## Response

Do not punish. Reach out, find the use case, and move it to a sanctioned plan with data-use terms you have reviewed. Expense data is the friendliest finding to act on because the user already told you what they use.
