# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems. Report them privately through
GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** form on this repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/NexusSecurityAdvisors/shadow-ai-detection-pack/security/advisories/new).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. You will get an
acknowledgement within 3 business days and a status update at least every 7 days until it is resolved.

## Supported versions

Only the latest commit on `main` receives security fixes.

## Scope

In scope: code, workflows and configuration in this repository. Out of scope: third-party
services and dependencies (report those upstream), denial of service, and social engineering.
